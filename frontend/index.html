Gold Jewellery,
Verified on Chain

Register authentic gold jewellery, track ownership, and record lifecycle events — with an immutable blockchain trail anyone can verify.

Connect your MetaMask wallet on Sepolia Testnet to register items, transfer ownership, and record events.

Look Up a Jewellery Item

Enter a registered item ID to retrieve its on-chain record, current owner, and full history.

Register a Jewellery Item

Authorized jewellers can register a new gold item. The item ID must be globally unique. Connect your wallet with a jeweller-authorized address.

Transfer Ownership

Transfers take two steps. The current owner proposes, and the new owner accepts from their own wallet. Ownership only changes after acceptance.

1. Propose a transfer (current owner)

The item must not be pledged. Proposing again replaces an earlier proposal.

2. Accept a transfer (new owner)

Switch MetaMask to the receiving account, then enter the item ID.

Record Lifecycle Event

Record a repair, alteration, valuation, or any significant event in the item's history. The current owner or an authorized jeweller can add events.

Pledge or Release Pledge

Mark an item as pledged as collateral. A pledged item cannot be transferred. Release the pledge when the loan is repaid.

Verify a Document

Select the certificate or invoice you hold. The file is hashed in your browser (it is never uploaded) and compared with the hash registered on-chain for the item.

Manage Jewellers

Only the admin (the wallet that deployed the contract) can authorize or revoke jewellers. Anyone can view the list.
